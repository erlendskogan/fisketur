Privacy Policy for Fisketur

Last updated 21 October 2026 · Norsk versjon

Who we are

Fisketur is made by Erlend Holth Skogan, who is the data controller for the app and this website. You can contact us at ersk0808@gmail.com.

In short

Fisketur has no user accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your game progress is stored only on your own device. The iOS app can show ads that you choose to watch. The ads come from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device when an ad is requested. The web version of the game has no ads.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress: your catches, in-game money, gear, how far you have come in the story and your settings. In the iOS app it also saves how many optional ads you have watched today, so it can keep to the daily limit. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

It is deleted when you delete the app, when you clear the website data in your browser (web version), or when you choose «Start et nytt spill» (Start a new game) in the game's settings.

Advertising in the iOS app

You can choose to watch a short ad in return for a reward in the game. Ads are always optional. They are provided by Google AdMob: Google LLC, or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland.

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates
  • information about your device and operating system, such as model, system version and language
  • device identifiers such as the identifier for vendors. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available, because the app never asks for permission to track you.
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them
  • crash and performance data for the ad software

The app requests non-personalized ads only. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell personal data. We do not receive any of this information ourselves, only summarized ad statistics from Google.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ads are requested. Where consent is required, we rely on it. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under «Dagbok og innstillinger» (Journal and settings) → «Personvern og reklame» (Privacy and ads). Withdrawing your consent does not affect anything that happened before.

The web version and this website

The web version of the game and this page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The web version shows no ads and stores your progress only in your browser.

Children

Fisketur is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app requests non-personalized ads only.

How long data is kept

We do not store any personal data ourselves. What the game saves on your device stays there until you delete it as described above. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, Google handles requests about advertising data, but you are welcome to contact us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet.

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Fisketur: ersk0808@gmail.com. Support: Fisketur support.
